# ROSTA Growth Capability Register

Status: Product backlog / approved concepts, not implementation acceptance

Baseline: `integration/rosta-release-candidate` @ `a96d0e05478bc2c61852fdf91bb46da1782030df`

Purpose: keep the agreed growth, retention, marketplace, sales-network, loyalty, personalization, B2B, and community capabilities in one authoritative backlog so future implementation waves can be scoped without losing product decisions made during planning.

## Product principles already agreed

1. ROSTA should evolve from a coffee marketplace into a marketplace + CRM + loyalty + subscription + personalization platform.
2. Roastery identity may be visible, but ROSTA should win repeat purchases by creating superior platform value rather than relying on hiding seller identity.
3. Public seller surfaces should avoid unnecessary direct-sales contact leakage such as phone, WhatsApp, direct-shop URLs, social sales links, or precise street address unless operationally required.
4. Seller fulfillment policy should distinguish normal product branding from deliberate off-platform solicitation such as direct-purchase cards, QR codes, coupons, or inserts intended to bypass ROSTA.
5. ROSTA should provide its own package insert/QR for review, loyalty, reorder, Taste ID, and retention.
6. Marketplace commission is primarily a seller-side/platform settlement concern rather than an added 15% buyer fee.
7. Standard marketplace commission target discussed: 15% of eligible seller-side order value, subject to future financial/legal validation and policy versioning.
8. Promotions, loyalty, referral, credits, commissions, and payouts must use authoritative rule engines, ledgers, idempotency, auditable events, and financial snapshots rather than mutable ad-hoc balances.
9. Revenue-share arrangements should not be lifetime obligations by default.
10. Any automatic recurring charge must only be enabled when the payment provider capability is officially verified; otherwise subscription cycles remain payment-request based.
11. AI recommendations must operate over real catalog/inventory/pricing tools and must not invent products or commercial facts.

# Capability backlog

## CAP-01 — Rosta Growth Network / Rosta Growth Partner

Official launch model for field sales and partner-led growth.

A Growth Partner can earn through three acquisition channels:

- B2C customer acquisition
- Roastery acquisition
- B2B account acquisition such as cafes, offices, hotels, restaurants, and organizations

Core product scope:

- partner profile
- referral code
- referral URL
- unique QR
- attribution
- lead ownership
- lead CRM and pipeline
- customer portfolio
- roastery portfolio
- B2B portfolio
- levels/ranks
- milestones
- performance bonuses
- commission ledger
- pending/approved/available/paid states
- payout history
- reversal on refund/fraud when applicable
- anti-self-referral and fraud controls
- sales analytics
- admin oversight

Initial commercial model discussed for commission-only partners:

### B2C
- Starter: 8% of qualified first-order net amount + 2% repeat for up to 6 months
- Growth: 9% + 2.25% repeat for up to 6 months
- Pro: 10% + 2.5% repeat for up to 6 months
- no lifetime customer revenue share

### Roastery acquisition
- limited-time share of actual ROSTA platform revenue generated by the acquired roastery
- milestone/activation bonuses only after meaningful revenue, not merely registration

### B2B acquisition
- limited-time share of actual ROSTA platform revenue generated by the acquired B2B account
- milestone bonuses tied to real revenue

Attribution principles:

- customer reward only for net-new qualified customers
- customer attribution: first qualified referrer
- B2B/roastery lead: first registered + verified lead ownership with inactivity expiry policy
- signup alone does not earn commission
- order commission becomes eligible only after required payment/fulfillment/delivery/fraud/refund gates

Future extension targets:

- baristas
- creators/influencers
- cafes
- students
- independent marketers
- city representatives
- affiliates/ambassadors
