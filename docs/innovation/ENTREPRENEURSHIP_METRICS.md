# ROSTA Entrepreneurship & Impact Metrics

Version: **1.0**  
Date: **2026-08-20**

## Purpose

ROSTA must be able to demonstrate real entrepreneurial impact without inflating vanity metrics. This document defines a durable measurement model for job creation, technology development, seller enablement, R&D intensity and commercial impact.

These metrics are not themselves proof of knowledge-based eligibility. They provide evidence of growth, capability and economic impact that can support company strategy, financing, grants, programs and future evaluations.

## Metric principles

- distinguish verified from estimated data;
- preserve source evidence;
- never double-count people, revenue or sellers;
- separate ROSTA employment from contractors and independent Growth Partners;
- separate marketplace GMV from ROSTA revenue;
- separate technology revenue from commerce/service revenue;
- report gross and net effects where refunds/cancellations matter;
- keep definitions versioned.

## 1. Employment metrics

Track at least:

- full-time employees;
- part-time employees;
- insured employees where applicable;
- contractors/freelancers;
- R&D personnel;
- engineering personnel;
- operations/fulfillment personnel;
- sales/growth personnel;
- apprentices/interns if formal;
- hires during period;
- departures during period.

Do not classify independent sellers, carriers or Growth Partners as ROSTA employees unless the actual legal relationship is employment.

### R&D headcount evidence

For each R&D contributor retain:

- role;
- employment/contract basis;
- start/end dates;
- technology candidate(s);
- evidence/commit/experiment references;
- approximate allocation of time where needed for internal R&D accounting.

## 2. R&D investment metrics

Track separately:

- engineering payroll attributable to R&D;
- contractor R&D spend;
- cloud/compute specifically used for R&D;
- datasets/licenses used for R&D;
- testing/lab/pilot expense;
- specialized hardware/equipment;
- university/research collaboration;
- external technical consulting;
- total R&D expense;
- R&D as a percentage of ROSTA operating expense;
- R&D as a percentage of ROSTA revenue where meaningful.

Accounting classification must be approved by the accounting policy in force; these labels are management metrics and do not independently establish tax deductibility or capitalization.

## 3. Technology-output metrics

For each technology candidate track:

- number of active R&D experiments;
- completed experiments;
- rejected hypotheses;
- ADRs;
- benchmark reports;
- algorithm/model versions promoted to production;
- patents/applications if pursued;
- technical publications/presentations where appropriate;
- internal reusable components;
- commercial technology SKUs/services;
- pilots/customers using the technology product.

A high experiment count is not a success metric by itself. Outcome quality and evidence matter.

## 4. Marketplace entrepreneurship metrics

Track:

- verified active Roasteries;
- activated Roasteries with at least one delivered order;
- seller retention;
- seller GMV;
- seller payout value;
- seller payout timeliness;
- number of regions/cities represented;
- product/batch diversity;
- percentage of seller sales attributable to ROSTA where measurable and lawful;
- B2B accounts enabled through ROSTA in future.

These show ecosystem impact but remain separate from ROSTA technology-product evidence.

## 5. Fulfillment and local economic activity

Track when ROSTA Hub/direct-delivery operations begin:

- orders processed by Hub;
- grinding work items;
- packaging work items;
- local deliveries;
- carrier spend;
- fulfillment staff/contractor roles;
- regional partner hubs where applicable;
- SLA performance;
- waste/rework reduction.

## 6. Growth Network impact

Track Growth Partners as an independent commercial network:

- active qualified partners;
- qualified customers acquired;
- verified roastery leads activated;
- verified B2B leads activated;
- approved commission value;
- paid commission value;
- reversal/fraud rate.

Do not report Growth Partners as employees unless legally employed.

## 7. Customer and product impact

Useful non-vanity metrics include:

- delivered orders;
- reorder rate;
- verified recommendation acceptance;
- refund/incident rate;
- support resolution time;
- freshness-related delivery performance;
- Taste Profile completion and subsequent recommendation quality;
- customer retention cohorts.

## 8. Financial truth metrics

Every business-impact report must distinguish:

```text
Marketplace GMV
Seller-owned amount / seller payable
ROSTA marketplace commission revenue
ROSTA service revenue
ROSTA technology-product revenue
Refunds/reversals
Partner/Growth commissions
Operating costs
R&D costs
```

Never report GMV as ROSTA revenue.

## 9. Evidence requirements

Every externally used metric should identify:

- period;
- exact definition/version;
- source system/query/report;
- generated timestamp;
- exclusions;
- whether audited/verified;
- responsible owner.

Where a number is estimated, label it as an estimate and retain the estimation method.

## 10. Quarterly Entrepreneurship Report

Future company reporting should be able to produce a quarterly package with:

1. headcount and job creation;
2. R&D team/spend;
3. technology milestones;
4. seller ecosystem growth;
5. fulfillment/local operations impact;
6. customer impact;
7. financial truth summary;
8. grants/investment/support received;
9. major risks/blockers;
10. evidence references.

This report should be generated from auditable systems, not reconstructed manually for an application.
