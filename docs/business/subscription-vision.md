# ROSTA Subscription Capability Vision

Status: PS0.1 approved roadmap business capability contract — not a claim of current implementation

## 1. Purpose

ROSTA's approved roadmap includes distinct subscription capabilities, including **Coffee Subscription** and **Discovery Subscription**. This document defines their business compatibility with the existing marketplace model without claiming that recurring commerce is already implemented or commercially active.

Subscription must not create a parallel commerce, inventory, payment, fulfillment or financial system.

## 2. Subscription is an orchestration layer

A subscription is not a replacement for the canonical order model.

Expected relationship:

```text
Subscription Contract / Plan
  -> Scheduled Purchase Cycle
  -> Real quote/payment decision
  -> Master Order
  -> one or more Roastery Sub-orders
  -> existing item/service/fulfillment lifecycle
```

Each generated order must remain a real order with its own price, inventory, payment, seller commitment, fulfillment, SLA and exception truth.

Automatic recurring charge must not be assumed. It may be enabled only if the payment provider capability and required customer consent/mandate are officially verified and supported. Otherwise a subscription cycle must use an explicit payment-request/checkout flow.

## 3. Coffee Subscription boundary

Coffee Subscription may represent a recurring plan driven by preferences such as:

- cadence/frequency;
- selected product or eligible product set;
- coffee style/origin preferences;
- budget range;
- favorite/eligible Roasteries;
- whole-bean default;
- optional grinding service preference;
- fulfillment/delivery preferences where supported.

A grinding preference still creates an Order Item Service at order time; it must never convert ground coffee into the authoritative inventory/product variant truth.

## 4. Discovery Subscription boundary

Discovery Subscription is a distinct roadmap capability focused on curated/exploratory coffee selection rather than merely repeating a fixed product.

Its future implementation may use:

- Taste ID/preferences;
- recommendation/discovery policy;
- diversity/rotation rules;
- budget constraints;
- availability/freshness truth;
- eligible seller/product sets.

It must not silently substitute, charge or commit inventory outside the customer's approved subscription/checkout policy.

## 5. Multi-vendor compatibility

A subscription cycle may source from one or multiple Roasteries. If multiple Roasteries participate, the generated purchase follows Master Order + Roastery Sub-order decomposition.

Subscription must not create hidden cross-seller inventory ownership.

Each valid paid Sub-order becomes a seller fulfillment commitment; subscription does not reintroduce a routine post-payment seller accept/reject flow.

## 6. Fulfillment compatibility

Each generated Sub-order may use:

- Direct Fulfillment; or
- ROSTA Fulfillment.

A launch operating policy may centralize eligible subscription orders through ROSTA Fulfillment without removing Direct Fulfillment as a supported capability.

ROSTA Fulfillment continues to require Chain of Custody and its normal operational boundaries.

## 7. Financial compatibility

Subscription must not bypass:

- authoritative quote/pricing truth;
- payment verification;
- Financial Ledger truth;
- refunds/reversals;
- commission/settlement policy;
- discount/promotion funding truth;
- Growth Partner commission rules if attribution remains eligible for the cycle.

GMV generated by subscription remains GMV; it is not automatically ROSTA revenue.

Subscription revenue, if ROSTA later charges a separate subscription/service fee, must be independently identified and recognized according to the financial/tax policy in force.

## 8. Privacy compatibility

Subscription preferences are customer data governed by `customer-data-governance.md`.

Roasteries receive only the subset needed to execute their own generated Sub-orders. Subscription preference history must not become an unrestricted seller marketing dataset.

Recommendation/discovery use of subscription data must be purpose-limited and policy-governed.

## 9. Partner Experience compatibility

Generic Partner Experience policy may later attach an approved sample/gift/coupon/insert experience to an eligible subscription cycle. No specific Partner is a Core subscription dependency.

## 10. Failure handling

Future implementation must define safe behavior for:

- unavailable/sold-out products;
- unavailable Roastery before a cycle is committed;
- failed payment;
- seller incident after a valid paid commitment;
- fulfillment closure/SLA problems;
- customer pause/skip/cancel;
- preference changes;
- partial multi-vendor failure.

It must not silently substitute products, charge the customer or create fulfillment obligations without an approved policy/consent model.

## 11. Ownership, boundary, escalation

| Capability | Owner / Accountability | Boundary | Escalation Path |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coffee Subscription | ROSTA Product | recurring coffee plan -> real orders | Customer Support/Product Ops |
| Discovery Subscription | ROSTA Product | curated cycle -> real orders | Product/Recommendation Ops -> Support |
| Product truth | Roastery | products selected into generated Sub-order | Roastery -> ROSTA |
| Payment | ROSTA | each generated purchase | Finance/Support |
| Fulfillment | mode-specific | generated Sub-order | Roastery or ROSTA Fulfillment -> Support |
| Customer preferences | Customer/ROSTA privacy relationship | policy-governed personal data | Privacy/Support |
| Financial truth | ROSTA Finance | GMV/revenue/fees/settlement/refund | Finance -> policy owner |
