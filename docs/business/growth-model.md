# ROSTA Growth Network Business Contract

Status: PS0.1 business architecture reference

## 1. Canonical Growth model

The official program name is **ROSTA Growth Network** and the participant role is **Rosta Growth Partner**.

The program has three earning engines:

1. B2C customer acquisition;
2. Roastery acquisition;
3. B2B account acquisition.

Attribution, eligibility, ledger approval and payout are separate concepts. No commission is payable merely because a code, link, QR or lead record exists.

Canonical financial flow:

```text
Attribution / Verified Lead Ownership
  -> Qualified Revenue Event
  -> Eligibility Evaluation
  -> Partner Ledger Entry
  -> Pending
  -> Approved
  -> Available
  -> Paid
```

Exception states:

```text
Rejected
Reversed
```

## 2. B2C acquisition

### Official partner tiers

The approved launch tiers are:

| Tier | Qualified new customers / month | First eligible delivered purchase | Repeat eligible purchases | Earning window |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Starter | 1-19 | 8% | 2% | 6 months |
| Growth | 20-49 | 9% | 2.25% | 6 months |
| Pro | 50+ | 10% | 2.5% | 6 months |

Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum are **not** ROSTA Growth Partner tiers and must not be introduced as alternate business truth.

A qualified B2C customer/event must be:

- net-new to the acquisition policy;
- paid;
- delivered;
- non-refunded for the applicable qualification window;
- non-fraud;
- non-self-referral;
- otherwise eligible under the active policy version.

There is no commission for signup, referral-code observation, cart creation, unpaid orders, cancelled orders, refunded orders or fraud/self-referral.

The higher first-purchase percentage is treated as customer-acquisition cost, not a perpetual revenue share.

## 3. B2C attribution

Supported partner identifiers may include:

- partner profile/code;
- `/r/...` referral URL;
- unique QR;
- other approved attribution signals.

Pre-purchase attribution may be persisted, but **the first qualified delivered purchase locks customer attribution**.

Canonical rule: **First Qualified Referrer Wins**.

Default pre-purchase attribution window: **30 days**, configurable/versioned by policy.

Existing customers are generally not reclaimable as newly acquired customers merely by applying a later referral signal.

Attribution data is purpose-limited and does not grant the Growth Partner access to unrestricted customer identity, account data or order history.

## 4. Roastery acquisition

No payout is earned merely for registering a Roastery lead or signup.

After an acquired Roastery becomes eligible/revenue-generating, the approved launch model is:

- **10% of ROSTA actual net platform revenue generated by that Roastery for 6 months**;
- at **2,000,000 Toman cumulative actual ROSTA net platform revenue**: **750,000 Toman** milestone bonus;
- at **5,000,000 Toman cumulative actual ROSTA net platform revenue**: another **750,000 Toman** milestone bonus;
- total milestone bonus cap under this launch rule: **1,500,000 Toman**.

Revenue share is calculated from actual ROSTA net platform revenue, not Roastery GMV.

## 5. B2B acquisition

For eligible acquired B2B accounts, the approved launch model is:

- **15% of ROSTA actual net platform revenue for 6 months**;
- at **2,500,000 Toman cumulative actual ROSTA net platform revenue**: **1,000,000 Toman** milestone bonus;
- at **5,000,000 Toman cumulative actual ROSTA net platform revenue**: another **1,000,000 Toman** milestone bonus;
- total milestone bonus cap under this launch rule: **2,000,000 Toman**.

B2B acquisition may include eligible cafes, offices, hotels, restaurants and organizations according to policy.

## 6. Performance bonus

Performance bonuses are based on attributable **ROSTA net platform revenue**, not marketplace GMV.

Approved launch thresholds:

| Attributable ROSTA net platform revenue | Performance bonus |
|---:|---:|
| 20,000,000 Toman | 500,000 Toman |
| 35,000,000 Toman | 1,500,000 Toman |
| 60,000,000 Toman | 3,000,000 Toman |
| 100,000,000+ Toman | 5,000,000 Toman |

Exact period/reset rules must be versioned in the active Growth policy before financial execution.

## 7. Roastery and B2B lead ownership

Roastery/B2B acquisition uses a CRM-style lead lifecycle such as:

```text
Lead
  -> Contacted
  -> Meeting
  -> Interested
  -> Onboarding
  -> Activated
  -> Revenue Generating
```

Canonical ownership principle: **first registered + verified lead**.

Lead ownership is time-bounded and requires active follow-up. A default operational window may be approximately 30 days, but exact release/inactivity rules are configuration/policy and must be versioned.

Duplicate detection and lead-release rules must prevent permanent or fraudulent lead capture.

## 8. Partner ledger

Growth Partner financial truth is append-only/auditable.

Canonical ledger states:

```text
Pending -> Approved -> Available -> Paid
```

Exception states:

```text
Rejected
Reversed
```

Entry categories may include:

- first-purchase commission;
- repeat-purchase commission;
- Roastery revenue-share commission;
- B2B revenue-share commission;
- milestone bonus;
- performance bonus;
- refund/fraud reversal;
- payout;
- audited admin adjustment.

Each financial entry must carry sufficient provenance such as:

- partner identity;
- source order/account/lead/event;
- policy version/effective date;
- calculation base;
- amount;
- eligibility/approval state;
- reversal relationship where applicable;
- payout reference when paid.

Business invariants:

- no off-ledger payable commission;
- no duplicate commission for one qualifying event;
- no lifetime revenue share by default;
- refunds/fraud may create explicit reversals rather than deleting history;
- payout uses Available ledger truth only;
- Partner UI exposes only its authorized portfolio/ledger state.

## 9. Anti-fraud and privacy

Growth Network must support controls for:

- self-referral;
- duplicate/fake identities;
- collusive/refund abuse;
- duplicate lead claims;
- suspicious attribution patterns;
- policy-window manipulation.

Fraud scoring may inform holds/review but must remain auditable and must not grant Partners unrestricted customer data access.

## 10. Loyalty boundary

Customer loyalty is a separate product capability (**Rosta Club / Loyalty**) and is not redefined by PS0.1.

Growth Network may trigger a policy-approved customer incentive after the first qualified delivered purchase, such as Club points, but:

- Loyalty owns the points/reward ledger and tier policy;
- Growth Network owns partner attribution/commission eligibility;
- Promotion/financial engines own discount and monetary truth;
- no Growth Partner tier name may be reused as a customer Loyalty tier by implication.

PS0.1 intentionally does not invent Loyalty tier names, point values or redemption economics.

## 11. Employment/commercial boundary

The launch Growth Partner relationship is intended as **commission-only independent participation**, with no mandatory fixed schedule solely by virtue of joining the program, subject to the actual signed agreement and applicable law.

A later full-time employment model may use legal base compensation plus variable compensation. Employment classification, payroll, insurance and tax consequences must be validated under the rules in force when implemented.

## 12. Ownership, boundary, escalation

| Capability | Owner | Boundary | Escalation Path |
|---|---|---|---|
| Growth policy | ROSTA | versioned qualification/rate/lead rules | Growth Ops -> Admin/policy owner |
| Customer attribution | ROSTA | First Qualified Referrer Wins + purpose-limited data | Growth Ops -> Support/Admin |
| Roastery/B2B lead ownership | ROSTA Growth CRM | first registered + verified lead, inactivity/release rules | Growth Ops -> Admin |
| Commission ledger | ROSTA Finance | append-only approved financial records | Growth Ops -> Finance |
| Partner payout | ROSTA Finance | Available ledger only | Finance -> Admin/policy owner |
| Partner conduct | Partner under ROSTA rules | own campaigns/leads/portfolio only | Partner Ops -> Admin |
| Loyalty | ROSTA Loyalty capability | customer reward/points truth | Loyalty/Product -> Finance where monetary effect exists |
